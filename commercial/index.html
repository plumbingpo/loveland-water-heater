Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as lukewarm water at the dish machine and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater paired with the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse at peak service. We verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and document every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

Your schedule comes first. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep downtime to a minimum so the kitchen opens on time.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect venting and draft, confirm adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

Each hour without hot water costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the dish room. For a salon, it empties the chairs.
